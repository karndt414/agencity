Balcony gardening for beginners

Start small, choose containers with drainage, and give plants enough light, water, and the right potting mix.

1. Check your light

Most vegetables and herbs need several hours of direct sun. Match the plant to the light your balcony actually gets.

2. Use containers with holes

Pick pots or planters with drainage holes so roots do not sit in water.

3. Fill with potting mix

Use a quality container potting mix made for pots, not heavy garden soil.

4. Water steadily

Container plants dry out faster than ground plants, so check them often and water when the top layer feels dry.

Easy beginner picks

  • Herbs: basil, mint, parsley
  • Leafy greens: lettuce, spinach
  • Compact crops: cherry tomatoes, peppers

Simple weekly routine

  1. Look for dry soil and wilted leaves.
  2. Rotate pots for even sun exposure.
  3. Remove yellow leaves and spent flowers.
  4. Harvest often to keep plants producing.
